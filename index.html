📋 Apps Script Setup (one-time):
1. Open your Google Sheet → Extensions → Apps Script
2. Delete any code, paste the contents of appsscript.gs from your repo
3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web app
4. Execute as: Me  |  Who has access: Anyone
5. Click Deploy → copy the URL → paste above
6. On first use, Google will ask you to authorise the script — click Allow
How shared schedules work:
1. Admin logs in → Generate Schedule → it is automatically pushed to Google Sheets
2. Every user who opens the site loads that same schedule — no action needed
3. Non-admins never need to generate; they just open the page and see it

Member data updates:
1. Edit your Training Record sheet (roles, statuses, new members, etc.)
2. Reload this page — member data is always fetched fresh on load
3. Or click "Sync Now" at any time without reloading
